# Stitch Design Prompt

Design a persistent floating invitation assistant for Zuber and Bisma's existing Islamic wedding invitation. Keep the existing page composition: formal greeting, couple artwork, Quran verse, families, countdown, ceremony details, RSVP and closing dua. The assistant is a separate overlay opened by a fixed chat launcher, available throughout scrolling after the invitation reveal completes. Keep it hidden during loading, invalid invitations, envelope opening and the ceremonial reveal; when the invitation is replayed, hide it again until the reveal completes. Design the launcher, animated chat panel and interaction states within the existing invitation context.

Match the current theme precisely: emerald #073a2a, deep green #002f22, muted gold #b28a4b, paper white #fffdfa and page background #faf8f5. Use Cormorant Garamond for restrained serif headings and Montserrat for readable body and controls. Great Vibes belongs only to the existing couple heading. Use subtle gold rules and small botanical accents consistent with the existing artwork. No robots, neon, gradients, glass effects or large decorative AI branding.

Use a 56px circular emerald launcher with a white message icon, a fine gold border and a subtle shadow, fixed at the bottom-right. Offset it 20px from the viewport edges on desktop and 16px plus device safe-area insets on mobile. Give it the accessible name "Ask about the invitation" and a matching tooltip on desktop hover or keyboard focus. Keep its position stable while scrolling. Do not auto-open the chat, pulse continuously, show fake unread badges or add unsolicited greeting popups. The existing audio toggle stays at the top-right; the chat must not overlap it. Add sufficient page-bottom clearance so the launcher does not cover final actions or RSVP controls when scrolled into view.

On desktop, clicking the launcher opens a compact panel directly above it, approximately 360px wide and up to 520px tall, constrained to the available viewport height. Use a paper-white surface, thin muted-gold border, subtle shadow and corners no larger than 8px. The panel header reads "Wedding Assistant" with a small message icon, clear-conversation icon and close icon. The body contains a scrollable conversation, suggested questions and a composer anchored to the panel bottom. Keep the surrounding invitation visible and usable; no desktop backdrop or page-scroll lock. Closing returns to the launcher and preserves the conversation during the current invitation session. Clearing conversation is a separate explicit action.

On mobile, open the assistant as a full-width bottom sheet with a subtle dark translucent backdrop. Use up to 80% of the available viewport height, adjusted when the on-screen keyboard opens. Include a visible close icon, safe-area padding and a fixed header and composer with only the conversation scrolling. The sheet must stay above the keyboard without clipping the input or send button. Hide the launcher while the sheet is open. Lock background scrolling while open and restore the invitation's scroll position on close. Tapping the backdrop, the close icon or pressing Escape dismisses the sheet. Focus stays inside the modal sheet and returns to the launcher on close. The guest can close the chat immediately to use RSVP.

Animate desktop opening with a gentle upward translation of 12px, opacity from 0 to 1 and scale from 0.96 to 1, originating near the launcher, over 220-280ms with ease-out timing. On mobile, slide the sheet up from the bottom while the backdrop fades in over 250-300ms. Closing reverses the transition more quickly, around 180-220ms. Keep animations calm, without bounce or dramatic zoom. Respect reduced-motion preferences with an instant transition or brief opacity fade. Opening and closing must not shift the invitation layout. Do not automatically summon the mobile keyboard; focus the sheet heading or close control first, and open the keyboard when the guest taps the question input.

Conversation bubbles have small corners, muted pale green for the assistant, and emerald with white text for the guest. Keep replies concise and readable, with clear speaker labels and safe map links when confirmed. Include accessible contrast, visible focus, 44px minimum touch targets and tooltips for icon commands. Suggested questions wrap naturally above the composer. Preserve the guest's draft and conversation when closing and reopening. When a new reply arrives, follow it only if the guest is already near the conversation bottom; otherwise offer a discreet new-message indicator so reading earlier replies is not interrupted.

Show mobile at 375px and desktop at 1440px with the existing invitation constrained to 672px. Also account for narrow 320px screens, short landscape viewports and mobile keyboard-open states. Keep headings compact, suggestions wrapping cleanly, and text readable with no clipping or horizontal scrolling. Accommodate long questions and multi-paragraph replies. Derive conversation height from the panel's available space rather than imposing a fixed height that pushes controls outside the screen.

Design these states: closed launcher while viewing the ceremony details and RSVP; desktop panel open; mobile sheet open; opening and closing transition frames; empty greeting; active conversation; pending reply; failed request with Retry; rate limit; unavailable venue; mobile keyboard open; and reopened conversation with preserved draft. Include single-event invitations where only Nikah or only Walima suggestions appear. The chat must not reveal details for events absent from the invitation. RSVP remains a separate form; chat messages cannot confirm attendance. If a reply offers "Go to RSVP", activating it should close the assistant and scroll to the existing RSVP section.
